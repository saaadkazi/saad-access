PROJECTOR SYSTEM

Build 7446 features an AES Bi-LED projector setup capable of switching between White, Yellow and Warm beam modes.

The custom projector delivers long-range illumination reaching up to 800 meters while maintaining excellent beam focus for highway riding.

Integrated Hexagon DRLs connect through Bluetooth and provide access to 142 customizable lighting modes through smartphone control.

CRYSTAL DRLs

One of the first implementations of fully app-controlled crystal LED indicators on a Suzuki Access build.

The system connects through Bluetooth and allows complete control over brightness, effects and animations using 142 programmable lighting modes.

Designed to create a distinctive visual identity both during daytime and nighttime riding.
