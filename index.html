Build 7446 is the result of years of experimentation, creativity and dedication. From custom lighting and carbon fiber parts to performance-inspired styling, every modification reflects a personal vision rather than following trends.

This scooter continues to evolve with every new upgrade. More than just a modified Access 125, it represents the journey of turning an ordinary scooter into something truly special.

Highlight Masterpieces

01

Custom Dual Projector Setup

Engineered for extreme performance and luxury appeal. The headlight unit was split open in a dust-free chamber to place dual AES Bi-LED lenses. The configuration has smartphone-enabled RGB DRLs that sync with neon accent modes.

Brightness 7200 Lumens
Voltage Draw 45W Custom Relay
02

Bespoke Air Suspension

Replacing the standard front forks with high-performance inverted Showa gold units, this modification uses a pneumatic air cylinder inside the chassis, enabling the builder to lower or raise the vehicle on demand.

Height Travel 60mm Adjustable
Component Material CNC Anodized Aluminum
03

High-Output Under-Seat Audio

A masterpiece of internal styling. The scooter's utility trunk was converted into a sealed acoustic fiberglass speaker chamber. Loaded with a dedicated amplifier and coaxial speakers, it delivers high decibel bass notes.

Max Output 400W Peak Power
Visuals Sound-Reactive LED Halo
